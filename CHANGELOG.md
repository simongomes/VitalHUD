## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-08

Initial release of Vital HUD.

This is the first package. Download it, place the `VitalHUD` folder in your server resources, and start the resource. The HUD sits in the bottom-left and shows health, armor, hunger, thirst, stress, stamina, and oxygen.

### Added

- Compact survival HUD for health, armor, hunger, thirst, stress, stamina, and oxygen.
- Live vitals from the player ped, game natives, and Qbox or QB state.
- Configurable components, meters, visibility, and stat sources in `config.lua`.
- Hides the default minimap health and armor bars while leaving the minimap visible.
